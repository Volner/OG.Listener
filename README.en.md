OG.Listener turns your Spotify listening history into a personal music library. Explore your favorite tracks and artists, find your first listens, and see how your listening habits have changed.

[Open OG.Listener](https://volner.github.io/OG.Listener/#/upload)

## Features

- **Listening map.** The more you have played a track or artist, the larger its artwork appears.
- **Early discoveries.** Find artists you started listening to before their audience grew significantly. Comparisons are available when historical data can be found.
- **Personal statistics.** Your top-3, play counts, listening time, and charts for your entire library, individual tracks, or artists.
- **Search and filters.** Search regardless of case or punctuation, and filter by genre, dates, play count, and listening duration.
- **Music while you browse.** The built-in player keeps playing after you close a track card.
- **Russian and English.** The interface follows your browser language, with a manual language switch.

Genres, audio, and audience history depend on coverage from external sources. Some songs have previews only, and historical listener counts are not available for every artist.

## Get started

1. Request your official listening history in [Spotify's privacy settings](https://www.spotify.com/account/privacy/).
2. [Open the site](https://volner.github.io/OG.Listener/#/upload) and upload the ZIP or JSON you received.
3. Switch between tracks and artists, open their cards, and explore your history.

All you need is a browser and your own Spotify archive. Statistics cover the entire uploaded archive, including listening activity from before your first visit to the site.

## Your library

Your archive is processed in the browser and is not uploaded to the server. Your library stays in the same browser profile between visits. On another device, or after clearing browser data, you will need to upload the archive again.

To refresh your statistics, use the Upload archive button. The new archive replaces your current library.
